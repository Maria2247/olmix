about us

Olmix Sp. z o.o. is a business partner offering advisory, organizational and operational support for companies operating across various sectors of the economy.

Operating with a long-term perspective, the focus remains on professionalism, transparent communication, and solutions tailored to the individual needs of partners and the specific nature of each project.

scope of support

Business advisory

Comprehensive support in needs analysis, strategic planning, and selecting solutions tailored to business objectives and project specifics.

Cooperation coordination

Strategic organization of communication and operational activities between all parties involved, ensuring seamless cooperation at every stage.

Support for commercial processes

Assistance in developing commercial relationships, managing sales processes, and executing activities targeted at sustainable growth.

Project and transaction support

Organizational and operational support in the execution of selected projects and activities related to the preparation and handling of transactions.
